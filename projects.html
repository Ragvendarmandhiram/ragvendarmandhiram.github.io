Executed Installation · Pune

The Actual Mall Setup

Five photographs of the completed pavilion at Nexus Westend Mall, Pune.

Front view: The illuminated pavilion in the Nexus Westend Mall atrium, Pune.

Atrium perspective: The lotus-topped dome, sculpted peacocks, and Nexus Westend entrance branding.

Peacock detail: Blue and green peacock sculpture above the floral details.

Lotus pool: Illuminated lotus flowers and lily pads in the blue pool surrounding the pavilion.

View from above: The circular pavilion, visitor bridge, and surrounding lotus pool.

The space

Arched openings and a red carpet entrance invite visitors into the pavilion. Lighting outlines the dome and architectural details, while pink florals and peacocks add color.

The details

Illuminated lotus flowers and green lily pads sit in a blue pool around the pavilion, enclosed by a gold perimeter railing. Open the photographs to explore the installation from different viewpoints.
